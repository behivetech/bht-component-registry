Template: design links. A component's `package.json` can declare `registry.design: [{ label, url }]` (the generator asks for a Figma URL); the site gains a Design tab with a card per link, Figma links optionally embedded (`site.embedDesigns`, off by default), and `site.design` links the design-system file from the tokens page.
